How to play

Rules

  • Pieces move exactly like chess, but only onto empty squares.
  • Instead of capturing, you shoot. A kill doesn't move your piece.
  • Each turn: move OR shoot, never both.
  • Gun range: pawn = its two diagonal capture squares, knight = its L squares (the bullet flies straight), king = the 8 squares around it. Rook, bishop, queen: along their lines, up to 4 squares (half their movement), line must be clear.
  • You can miss. Bullets hit whatever is in their path first, even your own pieces.
  • Classic: kings are bulletproof; checkmate (the siege) wins. MerciZero: shoot the king to win.

Controls

  • You start looking through your king. The board in the corner shows everything.
  • Click one of your pieces (in 3D or on the corner board) to jump into its point of view. Q/E cycle pieces.
  • Green dots: squares it can move to. Click one to move.
  • Red rings and diamonds: enemies in its gun range. Click to shoot. The hit chance is shown on hover. In first person you aim where you click: centre mass is safest.
  • Drag (or arrow keys / WASD) to look around. Scroll to zoom your sights. V toggles an overview camera. M toggles music.
  • Red-tinted squares are the current piece's field of fire.
