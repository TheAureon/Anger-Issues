Gunmate

Chess, but every piece is armed. You see the battle through their eyes.

Mode
Opponent
Rules
  • Pieces move exactly like chess, but only onto empty squares.
  • Instead of capturing, you shoot. A kill doesn't move your piece: your rook can snipe and stay on its safe square.
  • Each turn: move OR shoot, never both.
  • Gun range: pawn = its two diagonal capture squares, knight = its L squares (the bullet flies straight, not in an L), king = the 8 squares around it.
  • Rook, bishop, queen shoot along their lines but only half as far: 4 squares. The line must be clear.
  • You can miss. Long shots wobble. Bullets hit whatever is in their path first, even your own pieces, and can fly over short ones.
  • Pawns promote when they reach the far rank. Castling works as in chess.
  • Classic: check means a king is in an enemy gun's reach. You can't leave your king in check. No legal action while in check = checkmate.
  • MerciZero: anything goes. Shoot the king and win (shoot your own and lose).

How to play

  • You start looking through your king. The board in the corner shows everything.
  • Click one of your pieces (in 3D or on the corner board) to jump into its point of view. Q/E cycle pieces.
  • Green dots: squares it can move to. Click one to move.
  • Red rings and diamonds: enemies in its gun range. Click to shoot. The hit chance is shown on hover. In first person you aim where you click: centre mass is safest.
  • Drag (or arrow keys / WASD) to look around. Scroll to zoom your sights. V toggles an overview camera.
  • Red-tinted squares are the current piece's field of fire.
